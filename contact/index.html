Questions

Support, in plain terms.

Short answers about writing in.

What makes a good bug report?

What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — plus your iPhone model and iOS version. Two sentences and a screenshot beat a page of guesswork.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within two working days. Replies come from someone who works on the app, so a busy launch week can stretch that a little — but nothing disappears into a queue.

What happens to what I send?

Your address and anything in your email are used to answer you, and nothing else. No marketing list, no sharing. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Can I suggest a beach or a feature?

Please do. Beach requests and feature ideas shape what gets built next. Name the beach and roughly where it is, and we'll take it from there.
